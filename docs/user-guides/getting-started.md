# Getting Started with GovBid Pro

Welcome to GovBid Pro! Follow these steps to get from account creation to your first bid submission.

## 1. Create Your Account

- Sign up with your business email.
- Verify your email address and log in.

## 2. Complete Your Business Profile

Populate your profile to improve AI matching:

- Legal business name
- NAICS codes
- Socioeconomic certifications (8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, etc.)
- Past performance highlights

## 3. Connect Integrations

Optional but recommended:

- Google or Outlook calendar
- Stripe for billing
- Salesforce for pipeline tracking

## 4. Discover Opportunities

1. Navigate to **Contracts → Search**.
2. Apply filters (agency, NAICS, set-aside).
3. Save your search and enable alerts.

## 5. Build a Proposal

- Add the opportunity to your pipeline.
- Open the **Proposal Builder**.
- Select a template and run the compliance checklist.

## 6. Review & Submit

- Invite reviewers.
- Capture approvals.
- Export the final proposal package.

## Helpful Tips

- Upload your capability statement for AI enrichment.
- Schedule recurring digests for new opportunities.
- Use the BOM generator to model pricing strategies.
