# GovBid Pro Feature Guide

This guide highlights core capabilities across GovBid Pro. Use it to understand how data flows from discovery to award tracking and how different modules connect.

## Contract Discovery

- **Unified Search**: Query SAM.gov, state portals, and commercial marketplaces from one interface.
- **Filters**: NAICS, agency, set-aside, performance location, and response deadline.
- **Saved Searches**: Store custom queries and receive digest alerts.

## Opportunity Intelligence

- **AI Match Scores**: Score opportunities against your business profile.
- **Competitive Insights**: Compare incumbents, NAICS trends, and average award sizes.
- **Requirement Extraction**: Highlight mandatory clauses and deliverables.

## Bid Compilation

- **Proposal Builder**: Section-by-section assembly with templates.
- **BOM Generator**: Aggressive, competitive, and premium pricing tiers.
- **Compliance Checklists**: Track mandatory forms and certifications.

## Collaboration

- **Versioning**: Change tracking and reviewer comments.
- **Approval Workflows**: Enforce sign-offs before submission.
- **Team Roles**: Limit sensitive fields by permission.

## Analytics

- **Pipeline Views**: Track opportunities by stage.
- **Win-Rate Insights**: Compare award outcomes over time.
- **Revenue Forecasts**: Blend probability, value, and timing.

## Notifications

- **Deadline Alerts**: 7-day/3-day/1-day reminders.
- **Amendment Monitoring**: Stay current on solicitation updates.
- **Digest Emails**: Daily or weekly summary of matching opportunities.

## Integrations

- **Calendar Sync**: Google and Outlook support.
- **Payments**: Stripe-backed subscription management.
- **CRM**: Salesforce and QuickBooks connectors for pipeline tracking.
